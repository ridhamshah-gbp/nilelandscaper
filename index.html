Why Nile

We build for the ground we've got.

Manitoba's clay soil and freeze-thaw winters are hard on outdoor work that isn't built right the first time. We plan every patio, wall, and bed with that in mind — proper base prep, materials that can take the swing from −30 to +30, and drainage that actually moves water away from the house.

We handle jobs of every size, from a single garden bed to a full yard rebuild, and we're on call for snow clearing once the landscaping season wraps up.
